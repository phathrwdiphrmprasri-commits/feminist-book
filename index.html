ABOUT THE NOVEL

Little Women

❦

Little Women is a timeless story about four sisters—Meg, Jo, Beth, and Amy— who grow up together during the American Civil War.

Although they have different dreams, personalities, and struggles, their love for one another keeps them close through every joy and hardship.

Filled with warmth, family, friendship, love, and the courage to follow your own dreams, Little Women reminds us that growing up does not mean growing apart.

SECTION I

Setting

─── ❦ ───

Set in 19th-century New England during the American Civil War, Little Women mainly takes place in the March family home in Concord, Massachusetts.

The story follows the four March sisters as they grow up in a changing world, surrounded by family, friends, and the challenges of everyday life.

19th Century New England

SECTION II

Women & Dreams

❦

Little Women inspires women to embrace their individuality and choose their own paths in life.

Through the four March sisters, the novel shows that there is no single definition of success or happiness.

Whether pursuing a career, building a family, creating art, or following personal dreams, every woman has the right to define her own future.

The story encourages women to value their voices, support one another, and remain true to themselves.

SECTION III

Character Symbolism

Turn the pages to discover what each March sister represents.

01

PAGE I

Meg March

─── ♡ ───

Meg, the eldest March sister, represents family, love, responsibility, and contentment. She dreams of a happy home and a loving family rather than a life filled with wealth.

Through Meg, the novel shows that a meaningful life does not have to be defined by material success. Her journey reflects the value of love, family, and finding happiness in an ordinary life.

Family · Love · Contentment

02

PAGE II

Jo March

─── ♡ ───

Jo, the second eldest sister, represents independence, ambition, creativity, and determination. She is passionate about writing and dreams of becoming a successful author.

Jo refuses to simply follow the expectations placed on women of her time. Her journey encourages women to have their own voices, pursue their ambitions, and create their own paths in life.

Independence · Ambition · Creativity

03

PAGE III

Beth March

─── ♡ ───

Beth, the third March sister, represents kindness, gentleness, selflessness, and unconditional love. She is quiet and shy, finding happiness in music and spending time with her family.

Beth reminds us that a person's value does not have to come from fame or achievement. Her character highlights the beauty of kindness, generosity, and caring for others.

Kindness · Gentleness · Selflessness

04

PAGE IV

Amy March

─── ♡ ───

Amy, the youngest March sister, represents ambition, artistry, confidence, and self-development. She dreams of becoming an accomplished artist and wants to experience the wider world.

As Amy grows older, she learns to balance her ambitions with responsibility and maturity. Her story shows that pursuing one's dreams can take many different forms.

Artistry · Confidence · Ambition
